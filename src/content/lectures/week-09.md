What is actually known about when and how the *Zhouyi* was written, and the
further interpretive layers later readers built on top of it.

## Outline

- the *Zhouyi* as a layered, composite text (core plus Ten Wings)
- the range of proposed dates, and Shaughnessy's specific dating argument,
  attributed by name
- later interpretive traditions built on the text (Han numerology, Wang
  Bi's philosophical reading, the Wilhelm/Baynes translation) as receptions,
  not the source
