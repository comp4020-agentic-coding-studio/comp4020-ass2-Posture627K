## The question

Everything this semester built toward reading one sign system carefully.
Does that reading skill transfer to claims and systems you haven't studied?

## What this builds on

Every earlier week contributed one piece: construction (weeks 1–4),
structural relationships and arrangement (weeks 5–7), transformation (week
8), and sourcing discipline applied to text and image (weeks 9–11). This
week applies all of it together.

## What you should be able to do after this week

Critically evaluate a modern popular claim built on this course's sign
system — feng shui's use of the Houtian arrangement, "quantum yin-yang"
analogies, or similar — against primary or academic sourcing, the same
standard used throughout the semester. As a transfer task, apply the same
toolkit (construction rules, structural relationships, arrangement,
fact-versus-interpretation discipline) to a small sign system this course
never covered. The final project (due this week) is where that transfer
happens — see the [assessment page](/assessments/) for the brief.
