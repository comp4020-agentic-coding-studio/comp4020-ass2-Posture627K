## The question

A popular story claims this notation predicted binary computing. What is the
documented version of that story, and where does it stop being documented?

## What this builds on

With the notation (weeks 1–8) and its early textual and visual history
(weeks 9–10) established, this week tests a specific popular claim against
the sourced timeline.

## What you should be able to do after this week

The documented sequence: Leibniz drafted binary arithmetic independently by
1679; Joachim Bouvet sent him Shao Yong's Xiantian diagram (week 6) in a
letter dated 4 November 1701; it reached Leibniz on 1 April 1703; he
published on binary arithmetic within about a week of receiving it. How
independent Leibniz's own binary work really was is a genuine, still-live
scholarly debate — not settled either way. The popular version compresses
this into a single fact where academic sources present a documented exchange
plus an open question.
