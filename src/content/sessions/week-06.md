## The question

Given the eight trigrams and their structural relationships, what is one
documented way of arranging them into a full order?

## What this builds on

Week 5's relationships (inversion, opposition) are the raw material an
arrangement has to organize. Xiantian ("Before Heaven") is the first of two
arrangements this course covers.

## What you should be able to do after this week

The Xiantian arrangement is a Song-dynasty construction credited to Shao Yong
(1011–1077), not a transmission from antiquity — this is documented
historically (Anne Birdwhistell's study of Shao Yong), separate from the
older legendary attribution of the trigrams themselves to the culture-hero
Fuxi. Keep the two claims apart: who is said to have invented the trigrams,
and who actually built this particular arrangement of them.
