## The question

What is the smallest mark that can carry a distinction — and what happens
when you commit to exactly two possible values for it?

## What this builds on

Week 1 asked what a change-representing sign system needs. This week
introduces the smallest such unit: a single line, broken or unbroken.

## What you should be able to do after this week

The *Xici* (one of the Ten Wings commentaries) gives a documented generative
sequence: Taiji → Liangyi (the two lines) → Sixiang → Bagua. This week keeps
close to that sequence rather than treating Taiji as one settled cosmological
idea — later interpretations (Zhou Dunyi's diagram in week 10, popular
"yin-yang symbol" readings) are separate objects of study, covered on their
own terms later, not folded into this week's definition.
