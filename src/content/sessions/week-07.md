## The question

The same eight trigrams, arranged a second way: what changes, and why would
a system need two arrangements at all?

## What this builds on

Week 6 established Xiantian as one internally symmetrical ordering. Houtian
("Later Heaven") is a genuinely different logic over the same eight objects,
which is the point of contrasting them directly.

## What you should be able to do after this week

Houtian is correlative: it grounds the trigram-direction-season-family
associations directly in the *Shuogua zhuan* (met first in week 4), rather
than in Xiantian's numerological symmetry. Two arrangements, two different
organizing principles, same eight trigrams — that contrast is the week's
main content, not a detour into which one is "correct."
